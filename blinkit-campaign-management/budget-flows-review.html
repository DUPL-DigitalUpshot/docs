Blinkit campaign budget: two ways to run the time slots

For review and approval. Both keep each campaign within its daily budget, split by time slot. They differ in who stops the spending at each slot's share.

Example used throughout: Daily budget ₹200 Morning slot 06:00–10:00 · 40% Evening slot 18:00–22:00 · 60%

Flow 1: Watch spend Current

Automation never changes the budget in Blinkit. It reads what has been spent and pauses the campaign when the slot's share is used.

Safety net: Blinkit's own daily budget (as the team set it) is never changed, so Blinkit still stops the campaign at that amount for the whole day. The next day at 11:00, spend is compared with Blinkit's report and any over- or under-spend is alerted.

Flow 2: Set Blinkit's budget per slot Option

At each slot's start, automation sets the campaign's daily budget in Blinkit to the share allowed so far. Blinkit stops the campaign by itself at that amount.
