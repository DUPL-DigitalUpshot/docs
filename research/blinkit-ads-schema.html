Blinkit Ads

What's inside a Blinkit Ads report

Blinkit sends one workbook with a sheet per ad type, and every row has its own date. Most sheets measure sales, not clicks.

Month-to-date report: checked Dashboard export: checked Not loaded into our database yet

The sheets

The month-to-date report has up to five sheets; a sheet is left out when there were no campaigns of that type. The dashboard export, sent by email, repeats three of them under other names.

What every daily sheet shares

The centre holds what all four daily sheets have. Each outer circle shows what only that sheet adds; the bands show columns a few sheets share. Listing Spotlight spells the budget column total_budget.

The metrics

Blinkit's own column names. Metrics tagged calculated are worked out from other columns, so after you add rows together, recalculate them from the totals.

How long Blinkit keeps changing a day's numbers

Blinkit updates recent days for a while after they happen. Measured on settled data: one file per day for 1 Sep – 14 Oct, pulled on 15 Oct, compared with five earlier downloads.

day 0day 5day 10
  • Impressions and spend change only in the last 2 days, by a rupee or two (about 0.01% in total).
  • Sales and add-to-carts change for up to 7 days: totals grow 1–2%, a single campaign-day by up to a few thousand rupees, and 3–10% of changes go down.
  • From day 8 on nothing changed, across about 3,000 older campaign-days. So we propose to re-pull the last 10 days every day; dashboards would then always end on settled numbers.

Questions for Blinkit

The files don't answer these. Each needs an answer before the metric goes on a client dashboard.

  1. Which spend is billed? Estimated Budget Consumed, Claimables or served budget, and is GST on top? (Q6)
  2. Which brand do a manufacturer's ads belong to? Files name FIELD FRESH FOODS PVT LTD, not a brand. (Q21)
  3. Which export is the source of truth when the month-to-date report and the dashboard export disagree? (Q19)
  4. Show Blinkit's CTR, or none? It is based on reach, so it isn't comparable with Zepto's. (Q20)
  5. Can New Users be added across days? A user new on Monday may count again on Tuesday. (Q9)
