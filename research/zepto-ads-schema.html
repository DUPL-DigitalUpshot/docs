Zepto Ads

What's inside a Zepto Ads report

Zepto gives six reports for each ad type. All six have the same metrics; they differ only in how the numbers are broken down.

Sponsored Products: checked Sponsored Brands: checked Sponsored Display: not seen yet

The six reports

City and Page show the whole brand. They can't be split by campaign.

What every report shares

The centre holds what every report has: the same 12 metrics, plus brand ID and name. Each outer circle shows what only that report adds. The bands show columns that a few reports share.

The metrics

These are Zepto's own column names. Metrics tagged calculated are worked out from other columns, so after you add rows together, recalculate them from the totals.

How much of the spend each report shows

The Campaign report has the full spend. Some breakdowns cover less, and that is how Zepto reports it, not an error.

  • Keyword, Sponsored Products (61%): auto and category-targeted campaigns have no keywords.
  • Category and Product, Sponsored Brands (71%): banner clicks aren't tied to a product.
  • City (99.8%): a small share of traffic has no city.

Things to know when reading the numbers

Questions for Zepto

The reports don't answer these. Each needs an answer before the metric goes on a client dashboard.

  1. What is ROBAS? It can't be worked out from the other columns, and it is sometimes higher than ROAS, sometimes lower.
  2. Is Orders orders or units? It is higher than add-to-carts on 18 of 20 campaigns (e.g. 2,116 orders vs 1,344 add-to-carts).
  3. When do reach and new-to-brand fill in? Unique_reach, Unique_considerations and New_to_brand_user_percentage are always 0 so far.
